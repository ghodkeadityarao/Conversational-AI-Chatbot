AI Chatbot

How it works

  • Ask anything. Simple questions (the time, the date, weather requests, your name) are answered instantly by the server; everything else goes to Google's Gemini model.
  • Say "My name is ..." and I'll use your name for the rest of this chat.
  • Enter sends, Shift + Enter starts a new line. New chat clears the conversation.
  • Each visitor has their own private conversation, kept in server memory for a couple of hours and never written to disk.
